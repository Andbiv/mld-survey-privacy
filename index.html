Privacy Policy for MLD Survey

Effective Date: May 11, 2026 · Last Updated: October 4, 2026

MLD Survey is a field tool for counting wildlife. You tap a species button for each animal you see, and the app records where and when you saw it, and the route you covered. This page explains exactly what the app stores, why it needs it, and when anything leaves your device. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

The short version

  • Your survey data lives only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no MLD Survey server. I (the developer) cannot see your surveys.
  • Your survey data leaves the device only when you export or share it yourself, or when your own device backup includes it.
  • Location is recorded only while a survey is active, and the app asks only for “While Using the App” permission.
  • The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking code. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • Two features use the internet: weather (from the U.S. National Weather Service) and maps (Apple Maps). Both are explained below.

What the App Stores, and Why

Everything below is stored in a database inside the app, on your device. It is used only to run the app and to build the files you choose to export.

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or any advertising identifier. Crew names are only what you type in, and they stay on the device unless you export a survey.

Location

Why: a wildlife count is only useful if you know where each animal was seen and what ground was covered. That is the only reason the app uses location.

When: location is recorded only while a survey is active.

  • When you tap a species button, that observation is stamped with your current position.
  • When you open a survey to start counting, the app begins recording your route. Pausing the survey pauses the route, and ending the survey stops it. When no survey is active, the app records no location at all.
  • Surveys often run for hours from a vehicle or aircraft, with the screen off between sightings. While a survey is active, the route keeps recording even if the screen is off or you switch to another app, so the route doesn’t have gaps. iOS shows the location indicator in the status bar whenever this is happening, so you can always tell.
  • To save storage, the route keeps at most about one point every few seconds, and drops readings that are less accurate than about 100 metres.

Permission: the app asks only for “While Using the App” location access. It never asks for “Always”. You can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → MLD Survey. Observations will then be saved without a position.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan a “Share Setup” QR code from another device running MLD Survey. The code holds species names, colours and species lists, and nothing else. No photo or video is taken or saved. The camera is used only while the scanner screen is open, and only if you allow it.

When Data Leaves Your Device

Your survey data leaves the device only in these cases, all started by you:

  • Exporting or sharing a survey. The app builds files (CSV spreadsheets, KML map files, and a summary, packed in a ZIP) and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose where they go: Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files, or another app. Once you send them, they are handled by whichever app or person you sent them to.
  • Saving at the end of a survey. When you finish a survey, a copy of its export ZIP is saved in the Files app under On My iPad (or On My iPhone) → MLD Survey → Exports. It stays on the device. It only goes anywhere if you move or share it.
  • Your own device backups. If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or a computer, the app’s data is included in that backup, as with any app. That backup belongs to your Apple account, not to MLD Survey.
  • Showing a Share Setup QR code. The code on screen contains only your species and species lists, never observations, locations, or names.

Two features connect to outside services while you use the app:

  • Weather. When a survey starts, when it ends, and when you export it, the app asks the U.S. National Weather Service’s free public weather service (api.weather.gov) for conditions at the nearest weather station. To find that station, the request includes the survey’s location (to within about 10 metres) and the survey’s time window. No name, account, crew name, observation or device identifier is sent. As with any internet request, the weather service can see your network’s IP address. If there is no internet connection, as is common in the field, the request is simply skipped, and the survey is still saved without weather.
  • Maps. The map screen uses Apple Maps, which downloads map and satellite imagery from Apple for the area on screen. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy. Your observations and route are drawn on top of the map on your device. They are not sent to Apple.

No other internet connections are made by the app.

What MLD Survey Does Not Do

  • It does not require or offer an account or sign-in.
  • It does not send your surveys, locations, or crew names to the developer or to any server.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking code.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share your information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data brokerage.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Crash Reports from Apple

If you have chosen in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements) to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and basic usage and performance statistics for the app. These are collected by Apple, not by MLD Survey. They do not include your survey data, and they are not linked to your identity. They are used only to find and fix bugs. You can turn this sharing off at any time in the same Settings screen.

Your Controls

  • Pause or end a survey to stop route recording immediately.
  • Turn off location or camera access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Delete a survey in the app to remove its observations and route from the device.
  • Delete exported files from Files → MLD Survey → Exports.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its data from the device.

Because your data is never sent to me, there is nothing held on a server for me to look up, hand over, or delete. Files you have exported and sent to other people or apps are outside the app’s control.

Children’s Privacy

MLD Survey is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

If the app ever changes how it uses data, this page will be updated first and the “Last Updated” date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
