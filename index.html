Privacy Policy for MLD Survey

Effective Date: May 11, 2026 · Last Updated: October 4, 2026

MLD Survey is a wildlife survey application used to record and manage animal observations and related field data.

Information We Collect

MLD Survey may collect the following categories of information:

  • Wildlife survey records entered by the user
  • Date and time of observations
  • Precise GPS location associated with observations
  • App usage and interaction information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Crash reports
  • Performance data

This information is not linked to your identity.

No user account is required to use MLD Survey.

How Information Is Used

Information collected by MLD Survey is used to:

  • Record and organize wildlife survey observations
  • Improve app functionality and reliability
  • Diagnose crashes and performance issues
  • Support app features that rely on device positioning or location services

Location Information

MLD Survey uses precise location data to geotag wildlife observations and to record the route you travel during a survey.

Location access is used solely for geotagging wildlife observations, recording the survey route, and related survey functionality.

Route recording starts when you start a survey and stops when you pause or end it. While a survey is active, the route keeps recording even if the screen is off or the app is in the background, and iOS shows the location indicator while it does. No location is recorded when no survey is active.

The app asks only for “While Using the App” location permission, and location access can be disabled at any time through iOS Settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use collected data for advertising, profiling, or data brokerage purposes.

Information collected by the app is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Survey records, including observation locations and survey routes, are stored only on your device. MLD Survey has no user accounts and no server of its own, and your survey data is never uploaded to us.

Your survey data leaves the device only when you choose to export or share it (for example, sending a CSV, KML, or ZIP file through the iOS share sheet), or when it is included in your own device backups.

Two features contact outside services while you use the app:

  • Weather. To record weather conditions for a survey, the app sends the survey’s approximate location and time to the U.S. National Weather Service’s public weather service (api.weather.gov) when a survey starts, ends, or is exported, if an internet connection is available. No name, account, or device identifier is sent with this request.
  • Maps. The map view uses Apple Maps, which loads map imagery from Apple for the area being viewed. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.

User Controls

Users may:

  • Disable location access through iOS Settings
  • Delete app data by removing the application from the device
  • Control app permissions through iOS privacy settings

Children’s Privacy

MLD Survey is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
