MLD Survey Support

Help and contact information for the MLD Survey wildlife count app, for iPhone and iPad.

About MLD Survey

MLD Survey is a field tool for counting wildlife from a truck or the air. Tap a species button once for each animal you see, and the app records the species, the time, and your GPS position. While you count, it also records the route you cover. Afterwards you can review each survey with a map, totals, and animals per mile, and export it as spreadsheets (CSV) and map files (KML). It works fully offline, needs no account, and keeps your data on your device.

Getting Started

  1. On the Home screen, tap NEW SURVEY. Choose a ranch, name the survey, set the date, choose a species list, and tap START COLLECTING.
  2. Allow location access when asked (“While Using the App”). The app uses it to place each animal and to record your route.
  3. Tap a species button for each animal you see. Each tap is saved immediately with the time and your position.
  4. Tapped the wrong one? Tap the minus on that species to remove its most recent count. An UNDO / MOVE TO… bar appears for a few seconds, so you can put it back or move it to the right species.
  5. Use ⏸ Pause for breaks such as fuel stops. Pausing also pauses the route. Tap TAP TO RESUME to carry on.
  6. When you’re done, tap END SURVEY (SAVE & FINISH). A ZIP of the survey’s files is saved automatically in the Files app (see below).
  7. Open Review & Export Surveys from Home to see each survey’s map, totals and effort, and to export or share it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do the GPS indicator colours mean?

The indicator on the count screen shows how accurate your position is right now. Green means within about 20 metres, orange means within about 50 metres, and red means worse than that, no signal yet, or location access turned off. If it isn’t green, give it a few seconds with a clear view of the sky. Taps are always saved, even when the GPS is red. They use the latest position the device has, or are saved without a position if there is none.

Why aren’t my taps registering?

The count screen locks itself after a period of no taps, so a bumped screen in a vehicle or aircraft can’t add stray counts. When it’s locked, tap the big button twice to unlock. To change the timer, tap the lock button (🔒) on the count screen and choose Disabled, 20 seconds, 30 seconds, 60 seconds, or 2 minutes (the default). If the screen says SURVEY PAUSED, tap it to resume.

Does the route keep recording with the screen off?

Yes. While a survey is active and not paused, the route keeps recording with the screen off or another app open, so there are no gaps. iOS shows the location indicator in the status bar while this is happening. Pausing or ending the survey stops it.

How do I add my own species or ranches?

From Home, open Manage Species to add, rename, recolour or reorder species, and to build named species lists, so each survey shows only the buttons you need. Open Manage Ranches to add the ranches you survey. The ranch name is used to label surveys and name exported files.

How do I set up a second device the same way?

On the device that’s already set up, tap Share Setup → Show QR Code on Home. On the new device, tap Import Setup → Scan QR Code and point the camera at it. This copies species and species lists only. Surveys and counts are not included.

How do I export my data?

Open Review & Export Surveys and tap Export on a survey, or select several and tap Export Selected. You get a ZIP containing a summary, CSV spreadsheets of every observation (species, time, coordinates), KML map files of the observations and the route (these open in Google Earth and most GIS software), survey effort, and weather when it’s available. The iOS share sheet then lets you email it, AirDrop it, or save it to Files.

Where does the end-of-survey ZIP go?

Open the Files app and go to On My iPad (or On My iPhone) → MLD Survey → Exports. You can AirDrop or copy the file from there later without opening the app. It’s still on the same device, though, so it is not a backup.

How do I back up my surveys?

In Review & Export Surveys, tap Export All Surveys (Backup) and save the file somewhere off the device, such as email, a computer, or cloud storage. Do this regularly. Deleting the app deletes its data, including the Exports folder.

Can I use MLD Survey without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline, including counting, route recording, review and export. GPS works without cell signal. Two things use the internet only when it’s available: weather conditions for a survey (from the U.S. National Weather Service), and the background imagery on the map screen (Apple Maps). Without signal, your counts and route are still recorded and saved, and weather is fetched again when you export with a connection.

Why is there no weather in my export?

The weather service only provides about the last 7 days of station readings. If the device had no signal when the survey started or ended, and the survey is exported more than about a week later, there is no weather to include. Exporting within a few days of flying, with a connection, avoids this. Weather comes from the U.S. National Weather Service, so it is only available for surveys flown in the United States. Everything else works the same elsewhere.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no server, and survey data leaves the device only when you export or share it. The Privacy Policy explains exactly what is stored, why, and when anything is sent.

System Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS / iPadOS 15.1 or later
  • Location Services enabled for MLD Survey (for positions and the survey route)
  • A device with built-in GPS is recommended for field use. Wi-Fi-only iPads have no GPS chip, so pair them with an external GPS receiver or use a cellular model.

Reporting a Bug

If something goes wrong, please email andrew@bivins.org with:

  • What happened, and what you expected to happen
  • The steps that lead to it, if you can repeat it
  • Your device model and its iOS / iPadOS version
  • The app version (Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage → MLD Survey, or the app’s App Store page)
