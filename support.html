About MLD Survey

MLD Survey is an iPad-optimized application for recording wildlife observations in the field. Tap a species button to log an observation with a GPS-tagged timestamp, organize observations into surveys, and export the results as CSV or KML.

Getting Started

  1. Open MLD Survey and grant location permission when prompted — GPS is required to tag observations.
  2. Create a new survey from the Survey Management screen, or use the active survey.
  3. Tap a species button on the main screen to record an observation at your current GPS location.
  4. Use the minus button on a species to remove the most recent observation if needed.
  5. When finished, export the survey to CSV (data) or KML (mapping) from the Survey Management screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

The GPS indicator is red or yellow — what does that mean?

The GPS status indicator reflects current location accuracy. Red indicates poor or no GPS signal, yellow indicates moderate accuracy, and green indicates good accuracy. Move to an area with a clearer sky view if your indicator is not green, and wait a few seconds for the signal to stabilize before recording observations.

Why aren't my taps registering?

MLD Survey has an auto-lock feature to prevent accidental button presses in the field. If the screen is locked, double-tap to unlock. The auto-lock timeout can be configured (Disabled, 30s, 60s, 90s, or 120s) in the app settings.

How do I add custom species?

Open the Species Management screen to add, edit, or reorder species. You can also create named species lists in Species List Management and assign a list to each survey, so different surveys only show the species you need.

How do I export my data?

From Survey Management, select a survey and choose Export. CSV exports include all observation records with species, timestamp, and GPS coordinates. KML exports open in Google Earth and other mapping tools to visualize observation locations.

Can I use MLD Survey without GPS or internet?

MLD Survey works fully offline once installed — no internet connection is required. GPS is required only if you want to geotag observations; the app uses the iPad's built-in location services.

Where is my data stored?

All survey data is stored locally on your device. Data only leaves the device when you choose to export or share it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

System Requirements

  • iPad running iPadOS 15 or later
  • Location services enabled (for GPS tagging)
  • iPad with built-in GPS recommended for field use
